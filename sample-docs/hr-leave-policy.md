# Leave Policy

This policy explains the types of leave available to employees of Contoso Software Pvt. Ltd. and how to apply for them.

## Annual paid leave

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave is credited monthly at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward to the next year; any remaining unused days lapse on 31 March.

Interns receive 1 day of paid leave per completed month of internship. Interns cannot carry leave forward.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 8 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days. Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

## Maternity and paternity leave

Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children, and 12 weeks from the third child onwards. Male employees receive 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## How to apply

All leave must be applied for in the HR portal (portal.contoso.local) at least 7 days in advance, except sick leave. Your reporting manager approves the request. Leave taken without approval is treated as unpaid leave.

## Public holidays

The company observes 12 public holidays per year. The holiday calendar is published in the HR portal every December for the following year.
