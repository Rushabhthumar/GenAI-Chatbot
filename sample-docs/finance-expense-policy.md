# Travel and Expense Policy

This policy explains which business expenses are reimbursed and how to claim them.

## Submitting claims

Expense claims must be submitted in the Finance app within 30 days of the expense, with a photo of the original receipt. Claims older than 30 days are rejected. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Travel

Domestic flights must be booked in economy class through the company travel desk at least 14 days before travel. International flights longer than 8 hours may be booked in premium economy with manager approval.

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 per night in other cities.

## Meals and daily allowance

During business travel, employees receive a daily allowance of INR 1,500 for meals. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Client entertainment

Client entertainment above INR 10,000 per event requires written pre-approval from a director.

## Internet and phone

Employees working from home receive a fixed internet allowance of INR 1,000 per month, paid automatically with the salary. Personal mobile phone bills are not reimbursed.
