# IT Security Policy

This policy defines how employees must protect company devices, accounts and data.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and contain upper-case letters, lower-case letters and a number. Passwords expire every 90 days and the last 5 passwords cannot be reused. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) with the Microsoft Authenticator app is mandatory for all accounts.

To reset a forgotten password, use the self-service portal at reset.contoso.local or call the IT helpdesk on extension 4444.

## VPN and remote work

The company VPN (GlobalProtect) must be used when working from outside the office network. VPN on personal mobile phones is NOT allowed; only company-managed laptops and phones enrolled in Intune may connect to the VPN.

## Laptops and devices

Company laptops are encrypted with BitLocker. Do not install software that is not in the Company Portal app. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 2 hours so the device can be wiped remotely.

## Email and phishing

Never open attachments from unknown senders. Report suspicious emails with the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook. The IT team runs a simulated phishing test every quarter; employees who fail it must complete a 30-minute security training.

## Data classification

Documents are classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Confidential and Restricted documents must not be uploaded to personal cloud storage or shared with external AI tools.
